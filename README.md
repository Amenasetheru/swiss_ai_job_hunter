# Swiss AI Job Hunter V1

Swiss AI Job Hunter is an enterprise-grade AI platform designed to automate and optimize job searching in Switzerland.

## Primary Objectives

1. Help users identify highly relevant Swiss job opportunities and improve their chances of obtaining interviews.
2. Demonstrate production-grade AI Engineering skills through a complete portfolio project.

## Core AI Capabilities

- Intelligent job offer analysis
- CV-to-job matching
- Retrieval-Augmented Generation
- LangGraph agent orchestration
- Personalized application recommendations
- Swiss market knowledge retrieval
